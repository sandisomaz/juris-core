Does our DPA with Meridian Logistics actually cover the data flows in the vendor agreement?
Partially. The DPA POPIA s.21 covers processing by Meridian as an operator, but the vendor agreement §7.2 describes a sub-processor (a routing partner) that isn't named or bound anywhere in the DPA. That's a gap — the sub-processor has no contractual obligation to meet the same conditions.

Cross-border transfer is also worth checking: the vendor agreement references servers in the EU, which needs its own basis under POPIA s.72 — the DPA's clause here cites the old regulations, not the current guidance.
