DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA)

SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND JURISDICTION
This Data Processing Addendum complements the SaaS Master Agreement governed by the laws of South Africa and European Union GDPR.

SECTION 2. SECURITY MEASURES
Processor shall implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect data against accidental loss or damage pursuant to POPIA Section 19 and GDPR Article 28.

SECTION 3. INCIDENT RESPONSE SLA
Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of discovery of any Data Breach.

SECTION 4. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
Personal Data may be freely transferred to offshore cloud servers without additional data subject consent or safeguards.
